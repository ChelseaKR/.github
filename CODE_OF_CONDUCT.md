# Code of conduct

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Disagree about the work, not the person.

Several of these projects are about people with less power than the people likely to be
reading the source — renters, transit riders, patients, people out of work. Contempt for them
is not a difference of opinion to be debated in an issue thread. It is out of scope and will
be removed.

## Expected

- Take criticism of your code as being about the code.
- Say what you actually think about a technical decision, including that I am wrong.
  Bluntness is fine; contempt is not.
- Open the issue even if your English is imperfect, or if you are not sure it is a real bug.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, sustained hostility, personal attacks, demeaning language about the people a
project serves, publishing anyone's private information, sexualised language or imagery.

## Reporting

Email **ckellyreif@gmail.com**. Reports come to me and are not discussed in public issues.
You will get an acknowledgement within a week.

These are one-maintainer projects, so there is no committee and no appeal past me. If a report
concerns my own conduct and you would rather not raise it with me, GitHub's own reporting is
the route that does not run through this account.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) 2.1.
